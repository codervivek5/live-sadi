### 2. Business model kya hona chahiye?

Portal par 3 primary services rakho:

| Service                    | Revenue model             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Event Live Streaming       | Per-event charge          |
| Event Website & Invitation | One-time package          |
| Photo & Video Gallery      | Storage / premium package |

Future mein:

- Event organizers subscription.
- Premium customized event pages.
- Digital invitations.
- Private guest access.
- Recording and replay.
- Photographer/videographer marketplace.

### 4. Meri actual business strategy

1. First 3 months: Weddings + Personal Celebrations. Ek clear niche establish karo.
2. Next 3 months: College events aur corporate live streaming introduce karo.
3. 6–12 months: Event organizers, photographers aur venues ke saath partnerships.

### 5. Brand positioning

Agar tum Vivaha naam choose karte ho, toh ek problem hai: Vivaha naam wedding-centric lagta hai, jabki tumhara vision all-event platform ka hai.

Isliye broader brand name better rahega.

Positioning:

# Celebrate Every Moment.

# One Platform.

Discover. Create. Stream. Celebrate.

Mera final verdict: Wedding se start karo, lekin architecture aur brand ko all-event platform ke liye build karo. Isse future mein business expand karne ke liye dobara branding aur technology change nahi karni padegi.

Ek important sawaal: Tumhara primary business kya hoga — log apne events ko live stream karenge, ya log events discover karke tickets/bookings bhi karenge? Dono ka business model kaafi alag hai.
